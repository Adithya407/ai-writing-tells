# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-07)

First release.

- `SKILL.md`: writing workflow, copyedit mode for the user's own drafts, disclosure guidance, traps, and a review format.
- `references/patterns.md`: the 53 signs from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" page, as of November 2026.
- `references/research.md`: findings from about 20 papers, with grammar-level patterns 54 (participial clauses) and 55 (nominalizations).
- `scripts/scan_tells.py`: scanner for the measurable patterns, with text and JSON output.
